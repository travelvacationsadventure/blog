A simple way to plan your travel budget

Organise your costs by day, transfer and experience so your budget reflects the trip you actually want.

By Travel Vacation Adventure·5 Sept 2026·1 min read
In this articleSeparate the main costsCount the whole transferChoose your prioritiesLeave some flexibility← All stories

Separate the main costs

Begin with accommodation, transport between bases, everyday spending and planned activities. Keeping these groups separate helps you see what changes when you add a destination or choose a different room. Use current quotes for your own dates instead of relying on someone else’s past total.

Count the whole transfer

When comparing transport options, check what the quoted amount covers. A lower headline figure may exclude parts of the journey or require another connection. Consider luggage, pickup points and the amount of time involved as well as the price.

Choose your priorities

Decide where spending more would genuinely improve your trip. That could be a convenient location, a guide for a particular visit or an extra night in a place you enjoy. Once those choices are clear, it is easier to simplify the less important parts without feeling that you are missing out.

Leave some flexibility

Allow room for changes and small unexpected costs. Record bookings and what remains to be paid in one place, including cancellation conditions. Before departure, check the total against the number of nights and planned travel days so that nothing important has been counted twice or left out.
