What to pack for coast and hill country

A small, adaptable packing list for a journey with changing scenery and different kinds of days.

By Travel Vacation Adventure·9 Sept 2026·2 min read
In this articlePack for your actual plansThink in simple layersKeep a practical day bagLeave room to adapt← All stories

Pack for your actual plans

Start with the activities you have chosen rather than every possible activity on the island. Comfortable everyday clothing, a useful extra layer and footwear you already trust are a better foundation than a bag full of new specialist items. Lay everything out and remove things that solve the same problem twice.

Think in simple layers

If your route combines the coast with the hill country, allow for different conditions. Choose layers that work together and are easy to carry during the day. A light rain layer can be useful without taking over your luggage. Check the forecast close to departure so the final choices suit your dates.

Keep a practical day bag

Leave room for drinking water, sun protection and the things you need for the outing. Keep travel documents and small essentials organised so that changing accommodation does not mean searching through every pocket. A separate pouch for cables makes charging devices simpler at the end of the day.

Leave room to adapt

Choose clothing suitable for the places you intend to visit, including religious sites, and check their specific requirements. Ask your accommodation about laundry if you want to bring fewer clothes. Before closing the bag, lift it and imagine carrying it through a station or up to your room.
